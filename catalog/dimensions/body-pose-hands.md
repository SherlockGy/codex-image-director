# 身体、动作与手

## 动作的描述顺序

从整体到末端，逐层写，每层一句：

```text
动作线 / 重心 → 躯干（前倾、弧度、扭转）→ 支撑腿 / 动作腿 → 手臂 → 每只手的握法与接触点 → 头与视线
```

示例写法：

- 躯干：upper body leaning forward and pressed low toward the extended leg, back forming an elegant flexible curve, coiled and ready to release。
- 重心：weight on her planted left foot；shoulders and hips counter-tilted。
- 视线：her irises point directly at <目标>, so her gaze clearly meets it。

## 受力链

不寻常的动作，要写清力从哪里来、作用在哪里：

- 接触面：the sole of her shoe presses directly against the plain grip。
- 方向：pushing the <prop> forward with leg power。
- 这个动作是角色能力的展示时，写明道具上没有辅助结构：the <prop> has NO pedal, NO stirrup … bracing it with her foot is purely her own display of strength。
  - 原因：动作不合常理时，模型会自己补一个让它合理的结构。

## 肢体比例

- 等长约束：both legs anatomically equal in length；the bent leg has the same thigh length and shin length as the straight leg — if straightened it would be exactly as long。
  - 透视缩短或弯曲的肢体很容易被画短，写“各段相等”比写“看起来一样长”有效。
  - 用户说“一样长”时，先复述确认指的是哪种情况。
- 体型调整：more slender and elongated, slimmer thighs and calves, refined knees, delicate ankles, healthy natural anatomy。写“更细”时一定要同时写健康的边界。
- 用身体做尺度单位：
  - 步幅：feet about one and a half shoe lengths apart。
  - 身高：about 7.5 heads tall。
  - 距离：about one forearm's length from her face。

## 手

| 动作 | 写法 |
|---|---|
| 握笔 | holds a pen in a correct tripod grip |
| 拉弓弦 | index, middle and ring fingers hooked on the string, the arrow nock between index and middle finger, pulled back to the anchor point at her cheek |
| 握杆、握扶手 | grips the rail with all fingers wrapped around it |
| 撑地 | arm straight with the palm flat on the floor, supporting her lean |
| 指向 | index finger straight and extended, the other three fingers softly curled, thumb relaxed |
| 托下巴 | hand lightly supports her chin, fingers curved naturally along her jaw |
| 悬停不接触 | hovers above the fingertip with a clear small gap of air；the hand does not touch the <object> |

通用写法：exactly five fingers on each visible hand, normal-length fingers with rounded fingertips, natural knuckles, wrist aligned with the forearm。

## 常见失败与对策

| 现象 | 对策 |
|---|---|
| 只写“扣弦”“指向”，结果手去捏道具，或只比划不握持 | 每只手分别写握法、手指摆位、接触点和“不接触什么” |
| 弯曲的腿被画短 | 写各段长度相等，展开后等长 |
| 步幅过大，像弓步，腿显得短 | 用鞋长做单位写步幅，并写 hips stay at the same height |
| 动作不合常理，道具上多出辅助结构 | 写明道具没有该结构，动作是能力展示 |
| 编辑后手型仍然错 | 换一种手型描述，或把手改成更好画的姿势；连续失败就告诉用户 |
| 人和道具分开生成后拼接，接触不真实 | 有接触的人和道具必须在同一张图里生成 |
