# 光影、环境与物理

## 光

按角色逐个写，每种光写清落在哪、在哪里减弱：

| 光 | 写法 |
|---|---|
| 主光 | warm lamp key light on her face and blouse from the desk side |
| 轮廓光 | cool silver-blue moonlight rim on her hair and silhouette |
| 环境光 | cool sky fill；warm wood bounce on the blouse |
| 特效光 | the <effect>'s glow falls on her fingertip, cheek and chin, and fades before reaching the chest |
| 整体曝光 | natural exposure, controlled bloom, no blown-out highlights |

冷暖对比（冷色月光配暖色特效光）是最省力的氛围手段。

## 环境层次

```text
前景（可选）：是否允许出现、不许遮挡什么
中景：主要的结构物，写清结构和透视
远景：轮廓、雾、天空；用景深虚化，保证主体是焦点
```

- 背景太空：在远景补层次（山脊、建筑剪影、云海、雾带），保持虚化。
- 背景太挤：把东西推远推低，写 smaller and softer in depth of field, not overlapping her limbs or props。
- 前景太近：去掉，或换成远景，例如 the lower-left becomes open sky high above the world。

## 物理规则

按需写进 Physics 行：

- contact shadows under feet, hands and objects
- hair and clothing respond to gravity and wind
- nothing solid floats; only light and effects may hover
- consistent perspective with one vanishing point; straight window frames and evenly spaced shelves
- every shadow falls in the same direction as the sun
- the reflection accurately mirrors the object above it
- furniture has the correct number of legs, all touching the floor

## 能量与特效

- 集中在一点，不要满屏：a ring-shaped air shockwave and bursting sparks just ahead of the tip。
- 沿方向延伸：a straight light streak continuing the line toward the target。
- 限制程度：restrained / contained glow。要排除 excessive bloom 和 neon glow on the character。
- 涟漪类特效：thin translucent rings spreading like ripples on water and fading quickly。

## 常见失败与对策

| 现象 | 对策 |
|---|---|
| 辉光过曝、紫色过饱和 | 写 controlled bloom，并在 Avoid 里排除 oversaturated <颜色> everywhere |
| 镜头拉远后背景大片空白 | 远景补层次，同时控制主体占比 |
| 前景物离镜头太近 | 删掉或换成远景 |
| 结构物透视错乱（窗、梯子、书架） | 写结构（平行、等距、连续）和一致透视 |
| 特效糊成一团 | 给特效几何形状（环、线、棱）和位置 |
