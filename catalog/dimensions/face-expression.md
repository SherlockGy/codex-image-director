# 面部与表情

## 脸

- 按目标写实档描述脸的结构（参照 style 卡），不要只写 beautiful。
  - 理想化写实：exquisite, finely sculpted, idealized beautiful face, flawless luminous porcelain skin。
  - 二次元风格化：small oval face with a slim pointed chin, flat smooth cheeks, nose only a tiny point, mouth a thin line, no volumetric lips。
- 年龄感：成年角色写 young adult woman, about N years old, never childlike, no school uniform。
- 保留脸（编辑或改造时）：same face shape, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, same expression and head angle — it must clearly be the same person。

## 眼睛

逐项描述形状、眼睑、虹膜和视线，不给比例数字：

- 形状：horizontally long almond eyes, wider than tall, outer corners lifting slightly。
- 上眼睑：bold tapered upper lid line with a short upward flick。
- 虹膜：top portion covered by the upper lid, gradient darker at top and brighter at bottom, a soft rounded human pupil（不是猫式竖瞳）。
- 视线：irises shift toward what she is looking at；常带侧目，让眼神有方向。

## 表情

先写程度，再排除过度：

| 想要 | 写法 |
|---|---|
| 轻微思考 | brows drawn in only very slightly with the faintest crease, never annoyed |
| 新灵感 | brows released, one slightly higher, eyes darting upward with a spark, lips just parted |
| 喜悦 | bright delighted eyes, a fresh closed-lip smile, not shouting |
| 冷静专注 | cold focused expression, eyes locked on the target along the pointing arm |
| 灵动不媚 | quick, clever and curious gaze with a bright playful spark, never seductive |

## 常见失败与对策

| 现象 | 对策 |
|---|---|
| 写了比例数字（如“眼睛占脸高四分之一”），眼睛变圆、显得呆萌 | 改写形状、眼睑、虹膜遮挡和视线落点 |
| 情绪词被放大，如“轻锁眉头”画成生气 | 写程度 + never annoyed 这类排除 |
| 比喻被字面化，如“细长瞳孔”画成猫的竖瞳 | 不用比喻；写 soft rounded human pupil，并排除 cat-like slit |
| 眼神发直、呆滞 | 给视线一个明确目标；排除 blank stare |
| 编辑别处时脸也变了 | 锁定清单写脸的各个部位；改完对比；必要时局部贴回 |
| 成年角色显得幼态 | 写年龄和头身比；排除 childlike proportions, baby-like expression |
